**⚠️⚠️ THE BIG ONE: the guide's asthma section is entirely superseded, and this is the most consequential correction in the whole extraction project so far.** The guide follows **NICE CKS 2023**, reflecting the old **NG80** ladder — SABA alone, then add ICS, then LTRA, then LABA, then MART. That was **replaced in full on 27 November 2024** by the joint **BTS/NICE/SIGN guideline NG245**, which was itself **updated in November 2025**. Both dates are after the assistant's knowledge cutoff, so this was researched on the web rather than recalled. What is now written:
- **The overriding new rule, at every age: never prescribe a SABA without a concomitant ICS prescription.**
- **Two new terms** — **AIR** (anti-inflammatory reliever: ICS/formoterol taken *only* as needed) and **MART** (the same inhaler as maintenance *and* reliever).
- **Diagnosis in 5 to 16 year olds** is now an ordered objective pathway: **FeNO 35 ppb or more** is diagnostic; then bronchodilator reversibility (FEV1 up 12% or more); then PEF variability 20% or more over 2 weeks; then skin prick testing to house dust mite or IgE with **eosinophils above 0.5 x 10^9/L**; then bronchial challenge. **Under 5s cannot be tested** — treat with ICS and attempt objective tests at age 5.
- **Under 5s:** low-dose ICS as an 8–12 week trial → LTRA trial (with an explicit stop rule) → specialist. **No MART pathway exists for under-5s.**
- **5 to 11:** low-dose ICS + SABA → **MART preferred wherever the child can manage it** (low- then moderate-dose) → specialist; the conventional LTRA/ICS-LABA route is now **reserved for children who cannot manage MART**.
- **12 and over:** **as-needed AIR** → low-dose MART → moderate-dose MART → check FeNO and eosinophils (refer if either raised) → time-limited LTRA **or LAMA** trial → specialist.
- **Prescribing reality check from the Nov 2025 update:** the only inhaler licensed for MART in this age group is a **budesonide/formoterol DPI 100/6, licensed from age 6 to 11 — not 5**; any other ICS/formoterol for MART under 12 is **off-label**.
- Also captured: **LAMA is new**, digital inhalers are **not recommended for routine use**, FeNO monitoring is now considered at review in adults, and the **MHRA neuropsychiatric warning on montelukast** must be given.
The guide's **non-pharmacological** asthma content (action plan, immunisations, trigger avoidance including beta-blockers and NSAIDs, screening for anxiety and depression, peak flow meter, inhaler technique, review checklist) is unchanged by NG245 and was carried across in full.
